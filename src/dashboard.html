How to read these numbers
Outside traffic excludes local checks, configured operator-associated addresses, and private or reserved addresses. It can still contain benign scanners or unknown tests. Source IPs are not people. HTTP requests and FTP sessions are different units. Detection rules are indicators, not proof of a successful exploit. Health percentages describe sampled local responses, not independently measured uptime.
All time means all records currently in the derived index. It does not imply that missing or never-captured history can be recovered. First and last indexed timestamps are shown above.
